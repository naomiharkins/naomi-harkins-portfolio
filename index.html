SDSS Galaxy Data Workflow

SQL · Python · Jupyter
Question
How complete and reliable is a large galaxy survey sample, and which records are good enough to analyze?
Method
Wrote SQL joins, filters, and aggregations to pull 164,072 records, then cleaned and analyzed them in Python. Built a separate 62,879-record validation workflow and documented every assumption and correction.
Result
85.9% spectroscopic completeness. Progressive quality filters cut 2,497,140 source records to a 1,306-record dataset ready for analysis.

Data Center Energy Dashboard

Tableau · Excel
Question
Which U.S. region is the best fit for a data center that needs reliable, renewable power?
Method
Cleaned, reshaped, and validated 548,951 records across 63 authorities and 13 regions. Reconciled totals to the source and tested every calculated field. Built an interactive dashboard with comparison KPIs.
Result
Recommended the Northwest: 52.3% renewable share, +21.7M annual net balance, and surplus power in 77.6% of hours. Tradeoffs and limits are stated in the dashboard.

A/B Testing Decision Analysis

Excel · Tableau · statistics
Question
Should the business ship each change, or stop?
Method
Compared two experiments and tested whether each difference was statistically meaningful or just noise.
Result
One test showed a significant effect and one did not. Each recommendation came with its uncertainty, limitations, and next steps.

Website Performance Analysis: Grammys

Excel · XLOOKUP
Question
How did Grammys and Recording Academy site traffic differ, including on Grammy Awards days?
Method
Calculated pages per session, bounce rate, and average time on site for each dataset and for the two combined. Used XLOOKUP to bring mobile-visit data into the main table, returning 0 when a date was missing.
