# Business Profile Quality Assurance Audit

This case study turns a research internship deliverable into a concise QA portfolio project. I checked a creative agency's public information across **7 platforms** and documented **25 missing, inaccurate, or broken fields** across address, email, phone, hours, and website/link coverage.

## Approach

1. Defined a repeatable field checklist.
2. Reviewed the website, social profiles, and business listings.
3. Classified each field as complete, missing, inaccurate, broken, or not applicable.
4. Compared profile completeness, search visibility, and reputation signals.
5. Converted findings into prioritized corrective actions.

## Selected findings

- Google Business was the most complete profile in the reviewed set, but still lacked key information.
- Several profiles used incomplete locations or broken cross-platform links.
- The business had a 5-star Google rating from 3 reviews, but no review responses.
- Six website testimonials were available but not fully reused across public reputation channels.

## QA relevance

The work required consistency checking across sources, careful documentation of actual versus expected information, evidence-based prioritization, and clear communication to nontechnical stakeholders.
